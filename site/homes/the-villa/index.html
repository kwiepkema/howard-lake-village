Lake life

Start the morning on the water

With Howard Lake just outside, you can spend a morning fishing from the dock and come back for lunch at your own pace.

For time out on the lake, three kayaks are available to rent separately. Kayak rentals are not included with your stay.

Fire up the barbecue or gather around the lakeside fire pit; firewood is provided. The dock and fire pit are shared when The Manor is booked separately.

Good to know

Questions before your stay?

Quick answers about The Villa. Confirm current rates, pet details and booking terms when you enquire.

How many guests can The Villa accommodate?

The Villa sleeps up to six guests.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms are there?

The Villa has two bedrooms and two bathrooms.

Is The Villa pet-friendly?

The Villa is currently pet-friendly. Pet limits and fees apply, so confirm the current policy when you enquire.

Are the dock and fire pit shared?

Yes. The property dock and lakeside fire pit are shared between the two homes when they are booked separately.

Are kayaks available?

Yes. Three kayaks are available for separate rental at Howard Lake Village. They are not included with The Villa. Ask about rental arrangements when planning your stay.

How do I check availability?

Send your preferred dates and group size through the Check Availability form. An enquiry does not confirm a reservation.
