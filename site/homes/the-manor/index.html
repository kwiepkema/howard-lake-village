The larger stay

Room for a larger group

The Manor is the larger of the two Howard Lake Village homes, with three bedrooms and approximately 4,000 square feet of fully furnished living space.

The home is arranged over two levels, with the main living room, kitchen and sunroom on the main level, and additional living space and game tables on the lower level.

Room to settle in

Easy time together, with room to spread out

After a day of fishing, relax in the hot tub overlooking Howard Lake. Outdoors, the property also has barbecue grills and a lakeside fire pit, with firewood provided.

Whether you spend the day out exploring or stay close to home, there is plenty of room to settle in.

The Manor FAQ

What is The Manor?

The Manor is the three-bedroom townhome at 5540 County Rd 6 SW in Howard Lake, Minnesota.

What can we enjoy at The Manor?

Enjoy a hot tub overlooking the lake, pool, ping-pong and foosball tables, and two large stone fireplaces: one gas and one wood-burning. Firewood is provided.

Is the dock private to The Manor?

The dock is shared when The Villa and The Manor are booked separately. You can fish from the property dock; check current fishing rules before your trip.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. An enquiry starts the conversation; availability and booking details are confirmed separately.
