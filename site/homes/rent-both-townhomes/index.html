Rent Both FAQ

What does Rent Both mean?

It means reserving The Villa and The Manor together for your group: two neighboring homes with five bedrooms, four bathrooms and room for up to 14 guests.

Where are the townhomes?

The Villa and The Manor are at 5536 and 5540 County Rd 6 SW, Howard Lake, MN 55349.

What can we enjoy outdoors?

The property includes a dock for fishing, outdoor barbecue grills and a lakeside fire pit with firewood provided. Three kayaks are available for separate rental.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. Availability and booking details are confirmed separately after the enquiry.
