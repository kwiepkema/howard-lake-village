Our Howard Lake story

A setting worth sharing

After living here for many years, Joe and Colleen moved closer to the city to be nearer to family. Their connection to Howard Lake remained.

They still value the scenery, the lake, the gardens and the birdlife—from eagles and pelicans to loons, hummingbirds and woodpeckers. Hosting gives them a way to share that setting with travelers.

They share practical local information to help you plan your days, whether you want to explore the area or spend more time by the lake.
