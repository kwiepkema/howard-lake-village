A lakeside home base

Make room for time together

Choose The Villa for a two-bedroom stay or The Manor for three bedrooms and more room indoors. Reserve both for a larger group, with separate kitchens and living spaces in neighboring homes.

Outside, there is room to enjoy the lawn, cook on the barbecue grills and gather around the lakeside fire pit. Firewood is provided.

The lakeside setting

Stay close to the lake.

Cast a line from the property dock or explore the lake by kayak, with three kayaks available to rent separately. The dock and fire pit are shared when the homes are booked separately.
