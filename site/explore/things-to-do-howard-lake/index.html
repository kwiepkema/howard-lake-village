Start with the water

Spend time around Howard Lake

Fish from the property dock or rent a kayak to explore the water. Three kayaks are available for separate rental. Town parks also provide public boating and fishing access.

Parks and outdoor stops

Lions Park

A swimming beach, playground, public restrooms and boat launch.

Central Park

A playground, seasonal splash pad and pickleball courts.

Memorial Park

Another outdoor stop with a fishing pier. Check seasonal availability before visiting local facilities.
