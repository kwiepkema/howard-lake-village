Fishing at Howard Lake

Fishing on Howard Lake

The lake supports species including largemouth bass, black crappie, bluegill, northern pike, walleye, yellow perch and other panfish and bass categories.

Fish populations, seasons and regulations change, so check current Minnesota DNR information before your trip.

Time on the water

Make lake time part of your stay

Staying beside Howard Lake makes it easy to fish at your own pace, with your kitchen and living space close by when you want a break.

You can fish from the property dock, shared when the homes are booked separately. Check current conditions, fishing licenses and Minnesota DNR regulations before heading out.

Come back to the house between lake days

Choose The Villa for a two-bedroom stay or The Manor for more space and a lake-view hot tub. Three kayaks are available to rent separately and are not included with either home.

After a day of fishing, cook on the barbecue or spend the evening around the lakeside fire pit. Firewood is provided.

Bringing a boat?

Lions Park has a public boat launch, giving visitors another way to get onto Howard Lake. Memorial Park also includes a fishing pier for a simpler shore-based option.

Access conditions and boating requirements can change, so check current local and Minnesota DNR information before the trip.
