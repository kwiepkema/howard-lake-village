A different pace for the weekend

Howard Lake combines a small-town setting with time on the water. Fish from the property dock, visit a local park or take the day as it comes.

Howard Lake's small-town setting gives you room to slow down, with a comfortable place by the water to return to.

Plan as much or as little as you want

Spend time on the lake or explore the area, then come back for a barbecue and an evening around the fire pit. Firewood is provided, and three kayaks are available to rent separately.

Comfort when the day moves indoors

Both homes offer fully furnished living spaces. The Manor also has a lake-view hot tub, two stone fireplaces and game tables for evenings indoors.
